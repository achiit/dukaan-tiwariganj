About the studio

A small shop with a big memory for faces.

My uncle gave me his old film camera in 2009, and I have not stopped clicking since. I learned editing at night on a borrowed computer, one wedding album at a time, because I could not afford to hire anyone.

In 2012 I opened this studio with my cousin Vikas helping at the counter. Today many of our customers are the children of people we photographed back then. That is the best review I can ask for.

I still answer the phone myself. Tell me what you need, and I will tell you the price and the date honestly.

  • Fixed, written pricesThe quote you get on WhatsApp is the bill you pay.
  • Photographer and editor in one placeThe person who shoots your event also edits it.
  • Edits delivered on the promised date48 hours for photos, 7 to 10 days for wedding films.
  • Your files stay safeEvery project is backed up for two years.
  • Walk in, no appointment neededPassport photos and prints are done while you wait.
